Which of 1,831 Atari ST hard-disk adaptations run from GEMDOS and GEMDRIVE

The 8bitchip.info website lists 1,831 Atari ST games adapted to start from a hard disk. This project started each one from a GEMDOS drive: a folder that the ST sees as drive C:, file by file, with no disk sectors. Two serve it: the Hatari emulator's GEMDOS drive, and the SidecarTridge Multidevice's GEMDRIVE on real machines, where every fix is tried and what fails is reported. Most games run as they are. The others need one of four fixes.

Why some do not

Most adaptations open their files through GEMDOS, by name, and both Hatari's GEMDOS drive and the Multidevice's GEMDRIVE serve them. The Gamex launchers with GOS4 or GOS5 work differently. They bring their own GEMDOS, a copy of TOS 1.04's that runs from RAM and reads the disk sector by sector through a hard-disk driver. GEMDOS and GEMDRIVE have neither the driver nor the sectors, so these 204 games, Lemmings among them, stop at the start. They are most of the games to fix. Why Lemmings fails shows it step by step, and Verdicts and solutions gives the counts for every strategy.

Tested twice

Every game ran in Hatari, on an emulated ST and STE. Every fix then runs on real machines, an ST, an STE, a Mega ST and a Mega STE, with the SidecarTridge Multidevice's GEMDRIVE serving the games from a microSD card. Real machines find failures that Hatari does not show: a file that GEMDRIVE locks, memory that GEMDRIVE uses, a floppy drive that a game waits for. Each one is reported in the catalogue and in how the solutions are tested, with its cause once it is known.

The solutions

Four solutions fix the 212 games. Each one changes the Atari side only; the emulator stays as it is. 209 of the 212 games now run from GEMDOS and GEMDRIVE: Hatari's GEMDOS drive and the SidecarTridge Multidevice's GEMDRIVE.

  • S1 rebuilds the start program of the GOS games, so that GOS asks GEMDOS or GEMDRIVE for its files by name. 85 of the 87 GOS5 games run (the GOS5 games, built), and 117 of the 117 GOS4 games (the GOS4 games, built).
  • S2, S3 and S4 are small fixes for eight games, and 7 of them run (eight games with a small fix).

The project

It started with one game: the 2011 hard-disk adaptation of Lemmings, which stops at once when it is started from GEMDOS or GEMDRIVE. The first part of the work found out why, rebuilt the launcher from sources that reassemble to the original bytes, and gave every adaptation on the list a verdict. The second part builds the fixes, one group of games at a time, and tests each in Hatari and on the SidecarTridge Multidevice. Still to come: the games whose verdict is unsure, a check with acsi2stm's GemDrive, and a program that applies the fixes to a user's own copies.

The findings, in the order they were made: why Lemmings fails, the launch chain, the disk layer, the file formats, the launcher's signatures, the modes, recognising the modes from the files and the verdicts and solutions.

The adaptations, their list and their documentation come from a third party website. This site links to its pages and copies none of its files. Every run was made in Hatari 2.6.1 and SidecarTridge Multi-device with the Multidrive Emulator. Atari, ST, STE, TT, Falcon and TOS are Atari's names.
