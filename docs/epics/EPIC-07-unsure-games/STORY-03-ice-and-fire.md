## Goal

Find out whether Ice and Fire, three floppy images with no hard-disk version (PP's page: its disk
change detection is unusual), runs from the GEMDOS drive under a virtual floppy driver.

## Tasks

      licence is checked before it is used (C-03).
- [ ] Ice and Fire run with it from the GEMDOS drive, through a disk change.
- [ ] The verdict: fix, with the driver and the instructions, or not feasible, with the reason.

## Acceptance

Ice and Fire has a run under a virtual floppy driver and a verdict.
