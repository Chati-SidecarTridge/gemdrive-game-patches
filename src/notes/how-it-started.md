# How it started

I was annoyed when I found out that I not only had to use an ACSI hard disk device to play
Lemmings, but also a very specific hard disk driver. Why was that? I started digging into Lemmings
because I loved this game, and what I found was not very surprising.

Testing and refactoring all the games to support GEMDRIVE in my SidecarTridge Multidevice was
simply impossible a few years ago. But now, with the help of AI and agentic development, it was
time to let the agents perform a full test of all the games in Hatari's GEMDOS drive (the distant
cousin of my GEMDRIVE) and see what happens.
